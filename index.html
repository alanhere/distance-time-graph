🚶 Walk the Graph!

Tap Start
📍 Position–m
⚡ Velocity–m/s
⚙️ Settings and help
How to play
  1. Stick the marker on a wall at about camera height.
  2. Tap Start camera and point it at the marker. A green outline means it has been spotted.
  3. Stand a measured distance away (2 m is good) and tap Calibrate once.
  4. Tap Record, then walk! Away from the marker is +, towards it is −.
  5. Try walking fast, slow, speeding up, slowing down, and standing still. Can you predict the graph first?

Keep the marker in view and avoid very jerky movement. Velocity is the slope of the position graph over the last half second. Nothing leaves your phone: the camera picture is analysed right on it.
